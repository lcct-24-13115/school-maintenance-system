ANG MGA RULE
  Cancel (cancel_own)    Sariling ticket lang, may reason. Puwede lang bago ma-Assign:
                         Submitted, Pending Authorization, Approved. Waiting for User / Escalated ay puwede
                         lang kung hindi pa na-assign ang ticket kahit kailan. Assigned, In Progress, Work Completed,
                         Closed = hindi na.
  Confirm resolved       Requester lang, Work Completed lang -> Closed agad. Dumadaan pa rin sa closure rule ng model.
  Reopen (reopen_own)    Sariling ticket lang, may reason. Work Completed o Closed lang, at nasa loob ng
                         reopen_window_days (/admin/settings, default 14). Bilang mula sa completed_at (Work Completed)
                         o closed_at (Closed). Kasama ang mismong huling sandali. 0 = patay ang reopen.
                         Pagka-reopen: status = Reopened, nililinis ang completed_at at closed_at (nasa history ang dati).

Dagdag na hindi mo hiningi (para hindi ma-stuck ang Reopened ticket)
  Ang maintenance staff ay puwedeng i-ACCEPT TASK ang Reopened ticket (Reopened -> In Progress),
  at puwede itong i-reassign. Kung ayaw mo, burahin ang "Reopened" sa startWork(), assign() at TicketActions::REASSIGN.
