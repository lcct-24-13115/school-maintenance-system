ANG BAGONG GALAW (allowedNext)
  Submitted             -> Pending Authorization, Approved, Disapproved, Waiting for User, Escalated, Cancelled
  Pending Authorization -> Approved, Disapproved, Waiting for User, Escalated, Cancelled
  Approved              -> Assigned, Waiting for User, Escalated, Cancelled
  Assigned              -> In Progress, Waiting for User, Escalated          (hindi na puwedeng i-cancel)
  In Progress           -> Work Completed, Waiting for User, Escalated
  Work Completed        -> Closed, In Progress, Reopened
  Closed                -> Reopened                                          (dati: wala; ito lang ang pagbabago sa lumang status)
  Disapproved           -> (wala)
  Waiting for User      -> Submitted, Pending Authorization, Approved, Assigned, In Progress, Reopened, Escalated, Cancelled
  Escalated             -> Pending Authorization, Approved, Disapproved, Assigned, In Progress, Reopened, Cancelled
  Cancelled             -> (wala)
  Reopened              -> Assigned, In Progress, Waiting for User, Escalated

  CONFIRM WITH CLIENT (gaya ng iba): ang table na ito ang unang bersyon. Palitan sa TicketStatus::allowedNext()
  at sa MOVES ng test; sinadya na kailangang baguhin ang dalawa nang magkasabay.

Mga desisyon na dapat mong malaman
  - Waiting for User, Escalated, Reopened = "pending" (bukas pa ang trabaho): bilang sa Pending, puwedeng maging Overdue.
    Cancelled ay hindi pending at hindi completed (gaya ng Disapproved).
  - isTerminal() ay Closed, Disapproved, Cancelled (fixed list). Kaya ang saradong ticket ay wala pa ring notes/flags/files.
    Ang Reopened ay gagawin ng sariling action sa Phase 10, hindi ng karaniwang ticket actions.
  - Ang "cancel bago ma-Assign" na rule ay sa table (Assigned/In Progress/Work Completed ay hindi puwedeng mag-Cancelled).
    Ang "sarili mong ticket lang" ay trabaho ng policy sa Phase 8 (cancel_own).
  - Walang button pa para sa apat na bagong status. Sa Phase 8-10 sila dadagdag. Ngayon, tinitiyak lang na
    (a) tama ang label, kulay at tracker, (b) hindi sila nawawala sa board, dashboard, listahan at reports,
    (c) walang aksidenteng aksyon: hindi ma-approve/disapprove/send-for-authorization ang Waiting/Escalated ticket.
  - Sample data (php artisan db:seed) ay lumang 8 statuses pa rin (sadyang hindi ginalaw para hindi masira ang ibang tests).
